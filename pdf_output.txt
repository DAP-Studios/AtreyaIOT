About Atreya
Product & technology company in industrial automation
Atreya is a product engineering and technology company in the field of industrial 
automation, IoT and process monitoring. We design, manufacture and deploy 
smart instrumentation, gateways, energy meters and centralized management 
software that help industries digitize their operations, reduce losses and improve 
uptime.
Our portfolio spans the full IIoT stack —from field -grade sensors and

a cloud SCADA platform with real -time dashboards, alerts and analytics.
